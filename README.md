The new DUT gym E-application will allow the students to apply and pay for memberships in the comfort of their homes/residences. It is also a personalised fitness buddy to help students stay consistent and reach their fitness goals.
Deployed app : https://dutgym-web-22417848.azurewebsites.net/ or use "LoginformASPCore6.csproj.user" to access the project.
